Everyday Mathematics

🌍 3 Real-World Scenarios

We use equivalent fractions every single day without even realizing it!

👨‍🍳

1. Baking & Recipe Scaling

Chef Alex needs 1/2 cup of sugar. His measuring cup only measures in eighths (1/8).

He measures out 4/8 cups.

💡 1/2 and 4/8 hold the exact same amount!
🏃‍♀️

2. Running Track Laps

Maya ran 6/12 of a track loop.

Ethan ran 2/4 of the same loop.

Both covered exactly half the distance!

💡 6/12 = 2/4 = 1/2 distance!
🍕

3. Pizza Slice Party

Pizza A is cut into 4 large slices (you eat 3 slices = 3/4).

Pizza B is cut into 12 smaller slices (you eat 9 slices = 9/12).

💡 Eating 3/4 equals eating 9/12 of a pizza!
Grade 5 Core Math Concepts

💡 How Do Equivalent Fractions Work?

Equivalent Fractions represent equal quantities. To generate an equivalent fraction, we multiply or divide BOTH the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number.

✖️ Rule 1: Higher Terms (Scaling Up)

Multiply top and bottom by the same factor n.

23 × 44 = 812

➗ Rule 2: Lowest Terms (Simplifying)

Divide top and bottom by their Greatest Common Factor (GCF).

812 ÷ 44 = 23
