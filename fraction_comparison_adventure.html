What is a Fraction? 🧩

A fraction represents equal parts of a whole object.

Fraction Structure

Numerator vs Denominator ⬆️⬇️

3 4

⬆️ Top: NUMERATOR

How many parts we have / shade.

⬇️ Bottom: DENOMINATOR

Total equal parts in the whole.

Quick Memory Tip: NUMERATOR on Top, DENOMINATOR Down Below!

Comparison Symbols

Comparison Signs ⚖️

> GREATER THAN

Open mouth points left (eats the bigger fraction).

< LESS THAN

Pointy end points toward the smaller fraction.

= EQUAL TO

Both fractions have the exact same value.

🐊 Alligator Trick: The alligator's mouth ALWAYS opens wide to eat the LARGER fraction! 🍕
Rule 1: Same Denominators

Comparing 14 and 34

14 1 Slice Shaded
<
34 3 Slices Shaded

📌 Rule 1 Key takeaway:

When denominators are the SAME, look at the numerators. The fraction with the LARGER NUMERATOR is GREATER!

Therefore: 14 < 34

Rule 2: Same Numerators

Comparing 12 and 14

12 Half (Bigger Piece)
>
14 Quarter (Smaller Piece)

💡 Secret Rule for Same Numerator:

When numerators are the SAME, look at the denominators. A SMALLER DENOMINATOR means the whole was cut into FEWER, BIGGER pieces!

Therefore: 12 > 14

Rule 3: Super Trick

Cross Multiplication Method ⚡

Compare 23 and 34 using cross multiplication:

2 × 4 = 8
23 (Score: 8)
vs
3 × 3 = 9
34 (Score: 9)
Since 8 < 9, we quickly know that 23 < 34! 🎉
