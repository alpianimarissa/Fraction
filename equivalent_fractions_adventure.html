✨ The Golden Rule of Equivalent Fractions

To make an equivalent fraction, MULTIPLY or DIVIDE both the numerator (top number) and denominator (bottom number) by the SAME NUMBER (except 0).

A × k B × k
or
A ÷ k B ÷ k
