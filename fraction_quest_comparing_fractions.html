Lesson: Fraction Anatomy

Never Get Them Mixed Up! ⬆️⬇️

3 4

⬆️ TOP: NUMERATOR

How many slices we take or shade.

⬇️ BOTTOM: DENOMINATOR

Total ALL slices in 1 whole pizza.

💡 Memory Trick: NUMERATOR is ON TOP, DENOMINATOR is DOWN below!
Comparison Symbols

The Hungry Alligator Trick 🐊

> GREATER THAN

Mouth opens to the LEFT.

< LESS THAN

Mouth opens to the RIGHT.

= EQUAL TO

Values are identical.

🐊 Main Rule: The alligator's mouth ALWAYS opens wide towards the BIGGER fraction!
Rule 1: Same Denominator

Comparing 14 and 34

14 1 Slice
<
34 3 Slices

📌 Rule 1 Conclusion:

If the BOTTOM numbers (denominators) are SAME, look at the TOP numbers (numerators). The larger top number wins!

Therefore: 14 < 34

Rule 2: Same Numerator

Comparing 12 and 14

12 BIG Slice
>
14 SMALL Slice

💡 Same Numerator Secret:

The MORE SLICES DIVIDED (Bigger Denominator), the SMALLER each slice becomes! Imagine 1 cake shared between 2 people vs 8 people.

Therefore: 12 > 14

Rule 3: Super Trick ⚡

Cross-Multiplication Method

Compare 23 and 34 when both top and bottom numbers differ:

Multiply: 2 × 4 = 8
23 (Score: 8)
vs
Multiply: 3 × 3 = 9
34 (Score: 9)
Since score 8 < 9, the answer is immediately found: 23 < 34! Super fast! 🎉
